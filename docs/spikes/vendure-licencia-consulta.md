# Consulta escrita a Vendure GmbH sobre SaaS (borrador, NO enviado)

El ADR-0001 pide una respuesta escrita de Vendure GmbH sobre el uso de Vendure en un SaaS multi-tienda.
Enviarla es una comunicación externa en nombre del proyecto, así que la envía el propietario.
Dirección sugerida: el formulario de contacto de https://vendure.io/contact o el email comercial que figure allí.

---

**Asunto:** Licensing question: hosted multi-tenant SaaS built on Vendure 3.x (GPLv3)

Hello,

We are evaluating Vendure 3.8 as the commerce engine for a hosted, multi-tenant SaaS platform where independent
merchants each run their own store. We would not distribute Vendure to customers; merchants only use it through our
hosted service. Our own extensions would be separate plugins that use Vendure's public APIs.

Before deciding, we would like your written position on these points:

1. Does operating Vendure 3.x under GPLv3 as a hosted SaaS, without distributing it, require a Vendure Commercial License?
2. Your June 2024 announcement says the model "may introduce fees for certain types of commercial use", especially for
   "those offering SaaS solutions based on Vendure". Are such fees in effect today, and under what conditions?
3. If a commercial license is required or recommended for this use, which plan applies and how is it priced
   (per instance, per store/channel, or by revenue)?
4. Would patches to core services that we keep private and never distribute be acceptable under GPLv3 in this setup?
